Will tomorrow's air be unsafe?

Pick a day. For each of four CPCB stations in Chennai, the saved model predicts the chance that tomorrow's 24-hour PM2.5 exceeds India's limit of 60 µg/m³, using only what was known by the end of that day. The model runs in your browser; its numbers match the Python model exactly.
